URIAH POPP: FOUNDER CEO
18D COMBAT MEDIC

Before founding 6:8 Medical, Uriah Popp served as an elite medic within U.S. Army Special Operations and Special Mission Units. He managed massive hemorrhage, blast trauma, and airway emergencies in high-threat environments where traditional clinical systems were fundamentally unviable.

"In severe arterial trauma, a casualty can exsanguinate in less than 180 seconds. In the field, you don't have sterile lighting, a team of assistants, or second chances. You have your hands, your training, and the device in your kit. If that device is hard to pull, slips when bloody, or takes more than one hand to tighten, it's a critical failure."
